# Credits

ReVoice is a Metamod plugin originally by The Legion, maintained since by the ReHLDS community.

:::warning Deprecated

ReVoice is marked **Deprecated** by its maintainers and is not under active development.

:::

## How to help

Given its deprecated status, testing and bug reports through the [issue tracker](https://github.com/rehlds/ReVoice/issues) are especially useful. Include your ReHLDS version, `revoice.cfg`, and `rev status` output. Pull requests are also welcome.
